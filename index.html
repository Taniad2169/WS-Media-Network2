      Frequently asked questions

      How do I find a doctor or attorney near me?

      Choose Doctors or Attorneys, pick the specialty you need, select your state and type your city or ZIP code. The directory shows matching professionals with their phone number, website and address so you can contact them directly.

      Is it free to search the directory?

      Yes. Searching the WebSwan Media Network Directory is completely free, and you do not need to create an account.

      What types of doctors can I search for?

      You can search primary care doctors, pediatricians, OB/GYNs, cardiologists, dermatologists, orthopedic surgeons, psychiatrists, dentists, orthodontists, chiropractors and many more medical specialists.

      What types of attorneys can I search for?

      You can search personal injury and car accident lawyers, divorce and family lawyers, criminal defense and DUI attorneys, immigration lawyers, estate planning and probate attorneys, bankruptcy lawyers, employment lawyers, real estate attorneys, business lawyers and more.

      How do I choose the right lawyer for my case?

      Look for an attorney who focuses on your type of case, is licensed in your state and explains fees clearly. Many attorneys offer a free first consultation, so call two or three and ask about their experience with cases like yours.

      How do I choose the right doctor?

      Check that the doctor treats your condition, accepts your insurance, is accepting new patients and is convenient to where you live or work. You can confirm a doctor’s license on your state medical board’s website.

      Are the professionals in this directory verified?

      Each listing is added by our team, not by an automatic sign-up. We still recommend confirming a doctor’s license with your state medical board, or an attorney’s standing with your state bar, before you hire them.

      What if there are no results in my city?

      Try clearing the city or ZIP field to see everyone in your state, or choose a broader specialty. New doctors and attorneys join the directory regularly, so check back soon.

      I am a doctor or attorney. How do I get listed?

      Listings are part of WebSwan Media Network’s advertising plans. Scroll down to “Advertise with us”, fill out the short form or call (239) 667-0151, and our team will set up your listing.
